Privacy

Aperture has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Accounts are optional: without one, your boards live only in your own browser. If you sign in, they're also kept in your account so they sync between your devices. This page explains what that means in practice.

What stays in your browser

  • Pin boards — the photos, notes, links, threads and groups on your boards, and where you last left the camera, are saved in your browser's local storage.
  • Images you upload to a board are kept in your browser's IndexedDB storage. They are never sent to us.
  • Small preferences, such as the grid density and whether the minimap is shown.

If you aren't signed in, none of this is sent to our servers. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so use Export → Board file to keep a copy. Anyone who uses the same browser profile can see these boards.

If you sign in

  • Your account is your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name, and nothing else is requested. You can sign in without Google by email link instead.
  • Your boards and uploaded images are copied to your account, including the boards that were already in this browser when you first signed in, so they sync between your devices. Only you can read them: every board and image is locked to your account, unless you share a board (below).
  • Where it's stored. Accounts, boards and images are held by Supabase, which runs our database and file storage for us. We don't sell or share them with anyone else.
  • Staying signed in. Your sign-in is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie.
  • Signing out removes the copy of your boards from that browser. They stay in your account.
  • Deleting your data. Signed in, open the account panel on the board and choose Delete my cloud data: every board and image in your account is deleted straight away. To have the account itself (your email address) deleted too, ask us and we will do it.

What is sent elsewhere, and why

  • Searches go to our server, which forwards them to the Unsplash API and caches the results. We don't keep a record of what you searched for.
  • Photos load directly from Unsplash's image servers, so Unsplash receives normal web requests from your browser. When you download a photo, or export a board image that contains Unsplash photos, we tell Unsplash which photos were downloaded, as their API guidelines require. This report doesn't include anything about you.
  • Links and image addresses you add to a board load directly from the site they point to, which receives a normal web request from your browser.
  • Fonts and scripts load from Google Fonts and the jsDelivr CDN (including the sign-in library, when accounts are on).
  • Error reports. If the app crashes, it sends us the error message, a short technical trace, the page path and whether you were in grid or canvas view. Search terms, board contents and anything that identifies you are left out. We use these reports only to fix bugs.
  • Hosting. The site runs on Vercel, which handles every request and may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time, under its own policy.

Share links

Signed in, sharing a board gives it a short link (/b/…). Anyone who has that link can view the board as it is in your account, including later changes, until you stop sharing it, make a new link or delete the board. The link's preview in chat apps shows the board's name, how many photos, notes and links it has, and its first Unsplash photo. The link is random and isn't listed anywhere or shown to search engines, but treat it like the board itself. You can also require a password: we keep only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, and a password-protected link's preview shows nothing about the board.

Not signed in, the whole board is packed into the link itself, after the #. Browsers don't send that part of a link to servers, so we never see those boards. Anyone you give the link to can open the board.

Uploaded images are never included in share links of either kind.

Editing together

The owner of a board shared with a short link can let people edit it. Anyone with that link (and its password, if it has one) who signs in can then change the board, and their changes are saved into the owner's board. We record which accounts have opened the link for editing; that record is deleted when the owner turns editing off, makes a new link, changes the password or stops sharing. While you're on a board with others, they see a name for you (your Google name, the name you last commented with, or "Guest"; never your email address), where your pointer is and what you change. Pointer positions aren't stored. People who edit a board can't add images from their device, and never see the owner's uploaded images.

Comments

Signed-in people can comment on a board shared with a short link. A comment shows the name you type when you post it (we suggest your Google name or the first part of your email address, and you can change it), the time, and which card it's about. Anyone who can open the board can read its comments. We store which account wrote each comment so you can delete your own; your email address is never shown. The board's owner can delete any comment on their board. Deleting the board, or your account, deletes its comments.

Children

Aperture isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
